01 / THE PROBLEM

Make interacting systems feel like a game.

Movement, collisions, enemies, and gravity must respond together. A small change in one system can change the whole flight and combat experience.

My contribution

I contributed the core concept and enhanced the game for CSE423 coursework. The repository credits AlifKhan24 as the original creator; this project builds on that foundation.

Original creator: AlifKhan24 ↗ · Concept and enhancements: Md. Jahidul Hasan ↗

02 / THE APPROACH

A loop that keeps the world moving.

GLUT callbacks handle input, updates, and drawing. Python updates the simulation; OpenGL renders it. Player actions and environmental forces feed the next update.

03 / KEY DECISIONS

The details that shape the build.

01

Connect hazards to movement

Black holes attract, white holes repel, and wormholes relocate the player. Environmental objects affect navigation as well as the scene.

02

Offer two perspectives

First-person and third-person views give players different ways to judge position and threats.

03

Let challenge grow with play

Difficulty settings and progression affect combat. Upgrades and enemy scaling give continued play a changing rhythm.

04 / THE RESULT

An explorable combat sandbox.

The build includes flight, enemies, weapon upgrades, gravitational hazards, and score tracking.

A constraint worth understanding

Parts of the physics use an assumed frame rate. Measured elapsed time would be a useful next step toward more consistent simulation across machines.
