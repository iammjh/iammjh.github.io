MJH portfolio favicon set

Original geometric mjh_ monogram, drawn as SVG paths without a font dependency.
Charcoal background: #10110f
Warm white lettering: #ede9df
Orange terminal cursor: #ff7748

Editable sources:
- favicon.svg: rounded browser/Android icon.
- apple-touch-icon.svg: full background for the platform-applied Apple mask.
- maskable-icon.svg: full background and smaller foreground for Android masks.

Exports:
- favicon.ico: 16, 32, 48, and 64px PNG frames.
- favicon-16x16.png and favicon-32x32.png: small browser fallbacks.
- apple-touch-icon.png: 180px.
- android-chrome-192x192.png and android-chrome-512x512.png.
- maskable-icon-512x512.png: foreground fits the central 80% safe circle.

PNG exports are rasterized from the corresponding SVG at each target size.
Changing an SVG does not automatically regenerate the PNG/ICO files.
